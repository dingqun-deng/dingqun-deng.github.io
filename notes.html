1. Student Notes on Functional Analysis and Kinetic Theory

Written in 2016–2018, perhaps during seminars, without polish.

  • Sobolev Spaces I August 31, 2016
    Approximation by smooth functions; partition of unity; Green formula; extension theorem; trace and embedding theorems
  • Sobolev Spaces II September 14, 2016
    Poincaré and Hardy inequalities; differential quotients; dual spaces; time-dependent spaces
  • Spectrum Analysis of Bounded Operator September 3, 2017
    Resolvent; Fredholm operators; essential spectrum; perturbations; index theory; Riesz projection and functional calculus; eigenvalues via complex analysis
  • Spectrum Analysis of Unbounded Operator February 9, 2018
    Graph norm; adjoint of unbounded operators; Riesz projection and functional calculus; perturbation of Fredholm operators
  • Boltzmann Equation with Soft Potential: A Simple Case June 21, 2018
    Linearized collision operator L = −ν + K; properties of ν and K; spectrum of the cut-off L; local and global solutions with exponentially decaying data
  • One-Parameter Semigroups July 12, 2018
    Semigroups and generators; Hille–Yosida theorem; perturbation; inversion formula; dissipative operators
