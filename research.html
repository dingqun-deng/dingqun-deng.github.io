Research

Research Interests

My current research interest is the analysis of partial differential equations related to kinetic theory, especially the Boltzmann equation and related models such as the Landau equation and relativistic or quantum kinetic equations, including nonlinear analysis and fluid dynamics. The research topics include boundary theory, existence and regularity, wave patterns, spectral analysis, fluid dynamic limit, pseudo-differential calculus, semigroup theory, rate of convergence, etc.
